Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and published on Google Play by AvM ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Collection and Use

Our applications are designed with a privacy-first, offline-first approach. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers.

All data you enter or generate within our applications—such as notes, logs, schedules, settings, recipes, vehicle records, or attached files—is stored strictly locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions

Depending on the specific features of an application, it may request access to certain device capabilities solely for local functionality:

  • Notifications & Alarms: Used exclusively to deliver local reminders and timers scheduled by you on your device.
  • Camera & Storage / Media: Used only when you explicitly choose to attach photos, scan documents, or export/import local backup files (e.g., PDF, CSV, JSON, or database backups).

No media, documents, or backups are ever uploaded to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Our applications do not integrate third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking SDKs. However, the apps are distributed via Google Play, which may collect anonymous crash logs and basic diagnostics in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security & Deletion

Because your data resides locally on your device, you remain in full control of it at all times. You can delete your data permanently at any moment by clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features (such as cloud sync or monetization) are introduced. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
