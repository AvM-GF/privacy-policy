Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications (including GaRF and other utilities) developed and published on Google Play by AvM ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Local-First Architecture & Data Encryption

Our applications are built on a strict privacy-first, offline-first architecture. We do not operate backend servers to collect, store, or process your personal information.

All user-entered and generated data—including vehicle profiles, maintenance logs, fuel records, insurance policies, attached documents, photos, and fuel receipts—is stored exclusively locally on your device inside encrypted storage protected by SQLCipher database encryption and AES-256-GCM file encryption.

2. Device Permissions

The application requests sensitive system permissions solely for on-device functionality:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly locally when you capture photos of documents, vehicle records, or scan receipts (including on-device OCR processing). Captured images are encrypted and stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Storage & Media: Used only when you explicitly import files/images or export local backups and reports (such as PDF or CSV files).
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used solely to trigger local maintenance reminders, insurance expiration alerts, or timers scheduled by you on the device.

3. External API Requests (VIN Decoding)

The application does not transmit your personal data in the background. The only external network request initiated by the app's functional features occurs when you explicitly trigger a VIN lookup: in that case, only the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is sent via HTTPS to the official U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) public API (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) solely to retrieve technical vehicle specifications.

4. No Telemetry, Analytics, or Advertising

Our applications contain no advertisements, collect no usage telemetry, and integrate no third-party behavioral tracking SDKs. Google Play may independently collect anonymized crash diagnostics in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You have full physical control over your encrypted data. You can delete individual records inside the app, wipe all application data via Android system settings, or uninstall the app to permanently remove all locally stored databases and encrypted files.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect future app updates. The latest version will always remain available at this URL.
